BOW Sports Capital — Module 3, Lesson 1

You've just been hired as the GM of an expansion franchise. Your job: build a championship-caliber roster through free agency.

But you can't just buy the biggest names. You need to balance production, value, risk, and cost — just like a real front office.

  • Select 9 players to fill your roster with required positions
  • Navigate the salary cap — spending over $160M triggers luxury tax penalties
  • Use 8 Scout Tokens to reveal hidden player data
  • Maximize your Efficiency Score — it measures WAR, value, risk, age, and chemistry
  • Survive the Season Simulation — injuries and breakouts will test your roster
  • Hit the Green Zone (75+) to prove you can run a front office

Each game draws a random Owner Mandate that changes the rules. No two games are the same.
